# ⚖️ Legal Assistant Pro

**Legal Assistant Pro** is a Streamlit-based AI application that provides two main legal tools:

- 📄 **Contract Analyzer** — Analyze PDF contracts for legal risks and missing clauses.
- 💬 **Legal Q&A Chatbot** — Ask AI-powered legal questions based on jurisdictions.

---

## 🔗 Live Demo

👉 Try the live app: [Streamlit App](https://muhammedmed-legalasistans-pro.streamlit.app/)

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📄 Contract Analyzer
Upload a contract (PDF format) to extract and analyze:

- Contract summary
- Key provisions
- Risk assessment
- Recommendations

### 💬 Legal Q&A Chatbot
- Select a legal jurisdiction
- Ask questions related to real estate or general law
- Get AI-generated responses tailored to your selected region
